Hope and equal opportunity for every child

Life Aglow for Children Foundation reaches children and families that are easy to overlook: kids out of school, families displaced by fire and flood, and elderly widows left on their own. We show up with what they need and stay to help them rebuild.

  • Education

    School fees, books, uniforms and learning materials so children can stay in class.

  • Healthcare

    Access to essential care and basic health support for children who can't afford it.

  • Poverty relief

    Food, shelter and everyday essentials for families and elderly widows in hardship.
